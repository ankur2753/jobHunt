# Unslop: Cold DM & Networking Edition
Edit generated messages to remove AI patterns and sound like a real peer reaching out.

## Rules:
1. **Zero Chatbot Greetings:** Never use "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your profile and was impressed", or "I am writing to you today to". Start directly with the context.
2. **No Sycophancy:** Do not over-praise the recipient or their company ("Your groundbreaking work at X"). Respect their time by being direct.
3. **Sound Like a Peer:** Write casually but professionally, as one engineer to another. Keep sentences short.
4. **Ban AI Vocabulary:** Remove words like "delve", "crucial", "underscore", "pivotal", "testament", "tapestry". 
5. **Cut the Filler:** "In order to" becomes "To". "Due to the fact that" becomes "Because".
6. **No Fake Sign-offs:** Ban "I look forward to hearing from you" or "Best regards,". Use a simple "Thanks," or "Cheers,".
